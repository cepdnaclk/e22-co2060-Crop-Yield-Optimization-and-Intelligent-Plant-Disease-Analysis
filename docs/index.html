The Problem We're Solving

Farmers Grow the Rice.
Others Set the Price.

Four gaps this project was built to close — in plain words.

⛔
Big buyers control the priceA small number of large millers buy most of the rice. Farmers often have no better option than to accept whatever price they're offered.
⛔
No reward for growing moreRight now, nothing rewards a farmer for growing more rice than others nearby, even when they do everything right.
⛔
No shared recordsThere's no simple, shared record of how much rice each district actually grows — which makes it hard to plan fair support.
⛔
Disease spreads before anyone noticesPlant disease is usually spotted late, by eye, so it can spread to nearby farms before help arrives.

How You Earn Points

Beat the Average. Earn the Points.

It's simple: the more your harvest beats your district's average, the more points you earn. This is the exact formula the app uses behind the scenes.

points = 1000 × √( max(0, yield − avg) ÷ (best − avg) )
In plain words: match the district average or fall below it, and you earn 0 points. Match the district's best yield, and you hit the 1000-point cap. Everything in between scales up smoothly — small improvements still count.

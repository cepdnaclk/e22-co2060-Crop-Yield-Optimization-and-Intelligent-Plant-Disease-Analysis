A Digital Ledger for Sri Lanka's Rice Economy

Every Harvest Deserves a Fair Price.

Sri Lanka's rice trade runs on opaque gate prices set by large-scale millers, with no island-wide record of what farmers actually grow. AgriConnect replaces that opacity with a performance-based points system, AI plant-disease detection, and a live outbreak map — so subsidies follow real yield, and disease gets caught before it spreads.

The Operational Gap

Reactive Pricing. Invisible Yields.
Late-Stage Disease.

Four structural gaps this project was built to close, drawn straight from the problem statement in the project documentation.

⛔
Price Cartel BehaviourLarge-scale millers hoard stock to create artificial shortages and manipulate prices, and farmers sell to them anyway under immediate financial pressure.
⛔
No Performance IncentiveNothing currently rewards a farmer for out-performing their district — subsidies aren't tied to measurable yield.
⛔
No Island-Wide TrackingCrop production isn't tracked at scale, so there's no data foundation to plan subsidies or promote higher yields.
⛔
Slow, Manual Disease IDPlant disease is identified by eye and word of mouth, so outbreaks spread before anyone official notices.

What's Actually Built

Four Systems, One Ledger.

A single platform connecting farmers and agricultural officers, built on a shared record of harvests, disease reports, and points.

Farmer & Officer Portal

Role-based dashboards for registration, farm records, and harvest history — separate views for farmers and agricultural officers.

Yield-Based Points Engine

Every harvest is compared against district, crop, and season averages, converting real performance into subsidy points.

AI Disease Scanner

Upload a leaf photo, get a classification across six rice conditions with confidence, treatment, and prevention guidance.

Disease Spread Heatmap

District-level outbreak severity on a live map of Sri Lanka, tiered from "no reports" to "critical" as reports come in.

Flood risk lookup near a farmer's land Address autocomplete & geocoding Full English / Sinhala interface In-app messaging between farmer & officer
How a Farmer Earns

The Points Formula, Made Visible.

A harvest is scored against the average yield for its district, crop, and season. Beat the average, and points scale up toward a cap — this is the same formula run in the backend's points engine.

points = P_MAX × √( max(0, yield − avg) ÷ (max − avg) )

P_MAX = 1000. Drag the slider to see how one farm's yield converts into points relative to its district.

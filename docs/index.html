The Problem We're Solving

Farmers Grow the Rice.
Others Set the Price.

Four gaps this project was built to close — in plain words.

⛔
Big buyers control the priceA small number of large millers buy most of the rice. Farmers often have no better option than to accept whatever price they're offered.
⛔
No reward for growing moreRight now, nothing rewards a farmer for growing more rice than others nearby, even when they do everything right.
⛔
No shared recordsThere's no simple, shared record of how much rice each district actually grows — which makes it hard to plan fair support.
⛔
Disease spreads before anyone noticesPlant disease is usually spotted late, by eye, so it can spread to nearby farms before help arrives.

What AgriConnect Does

One App for Farmers and Officers.

Everything a farmer and an agriculture officer need, in one simple place.

Farmer & Officer Portal

Officers register farmers, manage their farms, and record harvests. Farmers view their own crop records, points, alerts, and reports.

Points for Good Harvests

Earn a participation bonus, plus extra points for yield above the district benchmark. Missing benchmarks leave points pending until they can be calculated.

Disease Scanner

Select a registered farm, upload a rice-leaf photo, and add location details and notes. View the predicted condition, confidence, treatment, and prevention guidance.

Disease Map

Explore district-level reports by disease and time period, including custom dates. Hover or tap a district to inspect its report count and disease breakdown.

AgriBot Assistant

A chat helper on every page, answering questions in Sinhala or English — about the app, or about farming.

Message Your Officer

Send inquiries with supporting documents. Officers review attachments and update the status from Pending to Reviewed or Resolved.

Crop Records & CSV Export

Filter harvests by year, season, and crop; sort by date or yield; select records for CSV export; and refresh points from the Crop Data page.

Searchable Harvest History

How You Earn Points

Every Harvest Counts. Higher Yields Earn More.

Harvest quantity is divided by farm acreage and compared with the district average for the same crop, season, and year. The highest district average supplies the comparison benchmark.

bonus = 200 × yield ÷ avg
extra = 1000 × √( (yield − avg) ÷ (highest − avg) )
points = round(bonus + extra)
Below the district average, a harvest earns the bonus only. Matching the average earns 200 points. The extra component applies only when yield meets the average and the highest district average is greater. There is no 1000-point cap; 1000 is the extra component's scale.

If the district benchmark is missing, the harvest is saved with points pending. A zero average earns zero points. The example uses sample benchmarks, not live records.
